**English**

Aqar Nova is a Saudi cloud platform for property management and real-estate brokerage offices, built to take the
paperwork, spreadsheets and manual follow-up off the office's hands.

- **Contracts and units:** rent and sale contracts, properties, units, meters and title deeds in one place.
- **Collections:** instalment schedules, SMS and WhatsApp reminders to tenants, and online payment links.
- **Accounting:** receipts and payment vouchers, automatic journal entries, trial balance and financial
  statements, owner statements and payouts.
- **Government integrations:** register contracts on Ejar straight from your office's account, and
  ZATCA-connected e-invoicing.
- **Portals:** owners follow their income and statements; tenants pay, request maintenance and sign their
  lease from their phone.
- **AI assistant:** answers questions about the office's data and analyses reports.

Arabic first with English, Hijri and Gregorian calendars, and every office on its own domain and its own database.
Try it free: aqarnova.com
